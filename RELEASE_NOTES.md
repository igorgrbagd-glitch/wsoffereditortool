### Improved
- New customers also get their Diary page (Girl.json, copied from the newest girl of the same rank) and their QuestSystem.json hero entry. Renaming, changing class or rank, and removing a customer update both files too.
- Shop tabs are ordered Deals For You, Blueprint Pack, Monthly Cards (was Catalog), Monthly, Weekly, Daily, and the editor always opens on Deals For You.
- Popup previews use the full width of the screen. Shop Promotions keep their offers and text labels on the right, with hover and click highlighting like the shop cards.
- Daily Login Rewards has a clearer Girl section: pick her from a list with her portrait (CharacterPortraits), with scale and position next to it.
- After an update, What's new also lists the earlier versions (folded) below the new ones.
- Bigger pack cards in Event offers.
- Shop tab cards no longer show the offer id and name above them; hover a card to see them.
- Cleaner look: grouped toolbar with an unsaved-files count on Save, foldable sections, matching fields, and coloured notices (green done, amber warning, red error) that fade out by themselves.
- Smooth animations across the editor; the Season Pass levels coast after a drag.
